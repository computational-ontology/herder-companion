Ontology 360 · companion

The open companion of La Constitución en la era digital (manuscript in draft)

Data, codebook and schemas of the book, published under CC BY 4.0 (data) and Apache 2.0 (code). Repository: computational-ontology/herder-companion. Site of the matrix: ontology360.it.

Status: in preparation. The matrix is a single-coder first pass (exploratory); no row is a canonical reading until it has been double-coded under the α policy. No DOI has been minted yet.

Reuse

Load the CSV or JSON with any tool; join constitutional units to Constitute by constitute_id and article, and to the Comparative Constitutions Project by systid. Every file validates against its schema in CI; a record that carries a normative claim fails the build.
